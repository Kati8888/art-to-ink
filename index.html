About the Artist

The Same Form.
A New Story.

Tattoo studio licences

Licences for tattoo artists & studios

All licences renew yearly and cover professional use on clients under the limits below. The licence includes the available colour and black-and-white artwork files and resolutions published for each selected design. It does not transfer copyright.

01 · ARTIST

Independent Artist License

€249 / year

5 designs per year chosen from the catalogue, for one tattoo artist. Includes the available colour and black-and-white files for those designs, in the published resolutions. For client tattoos by the licensed artist only.

02 · STUDIO

Tattoo Studio License

€499 / year

20 designs per year chosen from the catalogue, for up to 5 tattoo artists at one studio location. Includes available colour and black-and-white files in the published resolutions. No resale or redistribution of source files.

03 · FULL CATALOGUE

Full Catalogue Studio License

€1,500 / year

All published designs in the catalogue for one studio location, for all tattoo artists working at that location. Includes every available colour and black-and-white version and all file resolutions supplied for each design. Commercial studio use is non-exclusive; copyright stays with the artist.

Exclusive rights to a specific design are not included in these licences. They require a separate written agreement defining the exact design, territory, duration and rights.
