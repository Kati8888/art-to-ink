The artist behind the work

Art with a pulse.

I’m Kateřina Pohnetalová. My paintings explore colour, emotion, unusual forms and the stories that connect people. ART TO INK is a way to take that visual language beyond the canvas and into personal, wearable art.

The aim is simple: keep the spirit of the artwork, respect its original shapes, and create a starting point for a thoughtful conversation between the wearer and their tattoo artist.
